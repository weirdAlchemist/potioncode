Where I've brewed

Twelve years of building software and then learning how to break it. Newest first.

  1. LIS GmbH

    schedule4 years

    What I did

    • Test automation — setting up projects, defining the architecture, implementing the tests.
    • Test environments — definition, configuration and maintenance across virtual machines and build/test pipelines.
    • Test analysis — turning requirements into cases that actually catch something.
    • AI initiative — conception and implementation of AI-driven processes, from the initial test design all the way through to monitoring the results of nightly automated test runs.

    What I think

    Eight years of writing features taught me where they break. Testing is the same job from the other side of the mirror — and it turns out the other side is where the interesting problems live.
    "No software is bug free."
